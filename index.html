Clips Ranking Uploader

Clips Ranking Uploader is a desktop web app for short-form creators. It lets a creator connect their own TikTok account and post their finished vertical videos (for example top-5 ranking clips) from their computer straight to their TikTok profile.

How it works

  1. Open the uploader on your computer and click Continue with TikTok.
  2. Sign in to TikTok and authorize the app (basic profile info, video upload, video posting).
  3. Pick a video, write the title, choose who can view it, set comments/duet/stitch and the content disclosure, then click Post.
  4. The app uploads the file through TikTok's Content Posting API and shows the posting status. You can also send a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft.

The app only posts when you click Post. It never adds watermarks and does not store your videos on any server. You can disconnect your account at any time.
