Clips Ranking Uploader

Clips Ranking Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It posts the videos we produce to our own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API, so we do not have to upload each file by hand.

  1. The channel owner clicks Continue with TikTok and authorizes the app (basic profile info, video upload, video posting).
  2. The owner picks a finished video, writes the title, chooses who can view it, sets comments/duet/stitch and the content disclosure.
  3. Nothing is published until the owner gives explicit consent by clicking Post. The app shows the upload status and the account can be disconnected at any time.

The app only publishes videos created by Clips Ranking to the channel's own account. It does not collect data from other users and does not use trackers. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
