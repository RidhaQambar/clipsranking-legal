Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Clips Ranking Uploader is the publishing tool of the Clips Ranking channel (@clipsrankingforlife03). It publishes the channel's own videos to the channel's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API, only after the channel owner gives consent by clicking Post.

Data we use and store

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives basic profile information (display name and avatar, shown so the owner knows which account is connected) and an OAuth access token and refresh token. These tokens are the only data stored, they are kept only to post to our own account, and they are stored on the channel's own computer. The videos are read from the channel's own files and sent to TikTok when posting; they are not stored on any server by the app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. We do not collect data from other TikTok users. No analytics or third-party trackers are used on this website or in the app.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can disconnect the app at any time with Disconnect in the app, which revokes the token and deletes it from the computer, or in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps. To request deletion of any data or ask a question, email ridah0030@gmail.com.
